Privacy Policy for Professor Sudoku

Effective Date: October 12, 2026

Developer: Professor Sudoku

We believe that your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Professor Sudoku mobile application ("App"). Please read this policy carefully. By downloading and using our App, you agree to the data practices described herein.

1. What Data We Collect

Our application does not require user accounts, log-ins, or registration. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or personally identifiable information, including but not limited to:

  • Name, email address, or phone number
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, IDFA, advertising ID)
  • Gameplay statistics or puzzle progress
  • Device model, OS version, or hardware information

You can use our App completely anonymously without providing any personal information to us.

2. Local Device Storage

All gameplay data, including your current puzzle progress, high scores, and settings, is stored exclusively on your local device. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not uploaded to our servers or any cloud service
  • Is not shared with third parties
  • Is automatically deleted if you uninstall the App

3. App Permissions and Device Access

Our App only requests the minimum permissions necessary for core functionality. We do not request or access:

  • Contacts, calendar, or call logs
  • Location services or GPS data
  • Camera or microphone
  • Photos, files, or media library
  • Device identifiers or phone number
  • Sensors or motion data

4. Third-Party Services

Our App is built using Kotlin and does not integrate:

  • Third-party advertising networks or ad SDKs
  • Analytics or tracking services (Google Analytics, Firebase, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • Social media SDKs
  • Crash reporting or performance monitoring tools
  • Any other tracking or telemetry services

Google Play Store: Our App includes a feature that allows you to rate us on Google Play Store. This simply redirects you to the official Google Play Store page for our App. We do not track your ratings or collect any information from this interaction.

5. Voluntary Feedback and Support Communications

If you choose to contact us for support, report a bug, or request a feature:

  • You will be redirected to your device's default email client
  • We only receive information you voluntarily type in your email (your email address, name, and message content)
  • We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry and improve the App
  • We will never sell, rent, or share your email address with third parties without explicit consent
  • Support communications are retained only as long as necessary to resolve your issue, typically no longer than 90 days

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. Our App:

  • Does not require registration or account creation
  • Does not collect any identifying information
  • Is not intentionally directed at children
  • Complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar global privacy laws

If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete such information immediately.

7. Third-Party Links and External Services

Our App may contain links to external services (such as Google Play Store). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these services. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies separately before sharing any information with them.

8. Data Security

Since we do not collect, store, or transmit personal data to our servers, there is minimal risk of data breach from our services. Your locally stored game data is subject to your device's built-in security. We recommend:

  • Keeping your device updated with the latest security patches
  • Using device-level security measures (PIN, password, fingerprint, or face unlock)
  • Not sharing your device with untrusted individuals

9. Policy Updates and Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. We will notify you of material changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. Continued use of our App after changes become effective constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete from our systems. You can delete all locally stored game data by uninstalling the App from your device.
