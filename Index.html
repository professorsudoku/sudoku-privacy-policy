Privacy Policy for Sudoku Puzzle App

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

We believe that your privacy is a priority. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our Sudoku mobile application.

1. No Personal Data Collection

Our application does not require any user accounts, log-ins, or registration. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, gameplay statistics, or personal identifiable information (PII).

2. Local Device Storage

All gameplay data, including your current puzzle progress, high scores, and settings, is stored strictly on your local device. This data never leaves your device and is deleted if you uninstall the application.

3. App Store Ratings

Our app includes a feature prompting you to rate the application. This feature simply redirects you to the official Google Play Store. We do not track whether you rate the app, nor do we collect any data from your review.

4. Voluntary Feedback and Communications

If you choose to contact us for support, report a bug, or request a feature, you will be redirected to your device’s default email client to send an email.

  • Data Collected: We only receive the information you voluntarily provide in the email (such as your email address, name, and the content of your message).
  • Use of Data: We use this information solely to respond to your inquiry, troubleshoot issues, and improve the application. We will never sell, rent, or share your email address.

5. Third-Party Services and Analytics

Our application is built using Kotlin and does not integrate any third-party advertising networks or tracking SDKs. Anonymous technical data regarding app crashes and performance may be collected automatically by the Google Play Store (via Android Vitals) to help us improve app stability. This data is fully anonymised and cannot be used to identify you.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at your support email address.
